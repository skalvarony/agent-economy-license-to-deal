Your agent searches three marketplaces and proposes. It cannot pay.
You approve. Only then it pays: the exact total, nothing else.
Ask for the three best; pick one.
The shop verified the agent’s signature and shows who bought, and how.
Marketplaces get a console: every order, the payment behind it, a refund in one click.
At the venue the code is honoured; the shop and the agent see it at once.
Both sides keep the record; the evidence page cross-checks them.
