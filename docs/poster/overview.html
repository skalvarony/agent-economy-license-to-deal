Your agent · one per person

Finds the deal, never pays without you

  • A brain that decides — an OpenAI model when the key is set, a scripted stand-in otherwise. The page says which.
  • Knows you — budget, categories, refundable-only, how you like deals shown. Editable, with a live preview.
  • Searches all marketplaces and proposes the best one — or the three best — with the exact total the shop quotes.
  • Uses your coins (store credit) and promo codes when they help.
  • Signs every request (RFC 9421, ES256) with a key published at /profile.json.
  • Pays with a token, never a card number: a Stripe pm_… the shop charges on its own account.
  • Keeps the record — journal, approvals with channel, receipts; /evidence answers "I never bought this".
  • Tells the shop how you decided — proposer, channel, time — so the shop can show it to you.
The model has no tool that pays. Paying is a separate route that only your Approve can trigger, for that exact total.
