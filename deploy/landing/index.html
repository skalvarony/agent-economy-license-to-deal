License to Deal

Vouchers for local experiences in Prague, sold through marketplaces that an AI agent can buy from on your behalf, and only with your approval.

Three marketplaces sell spa days, tastings and activities. Each one speaks the Universal Commerce Protocol, so a personal agent can search them, compare, and check out, while the marketplace verifies who the agent is and that its customer approved the exact total. People can also buy directly.

The marketplaces

  • Dusk Deals wellness · refundable · 10% back in coins
  • Praha Pass food and activities · 5% back
  • Dawn Saver wellness, cheaper · non-refundable

For agents

Each marketplace publishes its profile at /.well-known/ucp. Requests must be signed; a total that changes after the customer approved it is never charged.

License to Deal is a team entry at the Agents 0.0.7 hackathon, Prague, 8 to 9 October 2026. Everything here is a demo: the shops, their catalogues, prices, ratings and reviews are made up, and payments run in Stripe's test mode, so no real money moves and no real card is ever charged. Accounts are created by the team for the team. Nothing is sold.
